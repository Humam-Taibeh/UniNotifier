# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want UniNotifier to be a friendly, harassment-free place for everyone who uses it or helps with it —
whatever their age, background, experience, gender, religion or nationality. We pledge to act and
interact in ways that make the community open, welcoming and respectful.

## Our standards

Behaviour that makes the community better:

- Being kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Python, Git or Telegram bots.
- Respecting different opinions and experiences, and giving and accepting feedback gracefully.
- Owning your mistakes and learning from them.
- Keeping other people's privacy: never post someone else's student ID, password, token, timetable or
  grades — and never your own in a public issue.

Behaviour we won't accept:

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Publishing other people's private information without their permission.
- Using the project to get into anyone's account but your own.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies in every project space — issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews — and when
someone is representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainer is responsible for clarifying these standards and may remove, edit or reject comments,
commits, issues and other contributions that don't follow them, and will explain why when appropriate.

**To report a problem, privately:** open a private report at
<https://github.com/Humam-Taibeh/UniNotifier/security/advisories/new> and start the title with
"Conduct". Only the maintainer can read it. Every report is reviewed and handled fairly and the
reporter's privacy is respected.

## Consequences

1. **Correction** — a private note explaining what was inappropriate. A public apology may be asked for.
2. **Warning** — a warning with consequences for continued behaviour.
3. **Temporary ban** — no interaction with the project for a set period.
4. **Permanent ban** — for a pattern of violations or serious harm to the community.
